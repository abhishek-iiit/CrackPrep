# Design Patterns syllabus

Classic Gang of Four patterns, grouped for the `design-patterns` course.

## 01 Creational

How objects are created when new and constructors are not enough.

- 01.01 Singleton
- 01.02 Factory Method
- 01.03 Abstract Factory
- 01.04 Builder
- 01.05 Prototype

## 02 Structural

How classes and objects compose into larger structures.

- 02.01 Adapter
- 02.02 Bridge
- 02.03 Composite
- 02.04 Decorator
- 02.05 Facade
- 02.06 Flyweight
- 02.07 Proxy

## 03 Behavioral

How objects communicate and assign responsibility.

- 03.01 Chain Of Responsibility
- 03.02 Command
- 03.03 Interpreter
- 03.04 Iterator
- 03.05 Mediator
- 03.06 Memento
- 03.07 Observer
- 03.08 State
- 03.09 Strategy
- 03.10 Template Method
- 03.11 Visitor
